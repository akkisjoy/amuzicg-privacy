1Data we collect

We collect no personal data on our own servers — we operate none. Everything below stays strictly on your device unless you explicitly interact with a network feature:

  • On-device music library: metadata read from Android MediaStore and external audio files opened via intents or file pickers. Your local music library files, tags, and album art remain on your device and are never uploaded.
  • App preferences & saved content: equalizer settings, playback preferences, sleep timer, favorites, custom playlists, and saved karaoke recordings ("My Singing"). All saved locally via DataStore/SharedPreferences. Same privacy protections apply across all supported mobile devices.
  • Feedback & support emails (optional): if you tap Feedback, your email app sends a message to amuzicg@gmail.com. Only what you explicitly type and send is received.

2Permissions we request and why

  • Audio and media storage access (READ_MEDIA_AUDIO / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE on older Android): to list, play, and organize local audio files stored on your device and external storage.
  • Microphone (optional, RECORD_AUDIO): used for the on-device live audio visualizer and optional Karaoke singing mode. In Karaoke mode, your voice is temporarily recorded locally on-device for live pitch visualizer and vocal preview. If you tap Save, your mixed track is stored locally under "My Singing". If you tap Discard or Retry, temporary recordings are permanently removed. Microphone audio is never transmitted off your device or uploaded to any server.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): to display playback controls in the notification bar and deliver optional push notifications for live radio broadcasts, station alerts, and feature updates.
  • Foreground service & system wake lock (FOREGROUND_SERVICE*, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED, WAKE_LOCK): to keep media playback running uninterrupted in the background.
  • Internet & network state (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): for streaming internet radio, fetching public station directories, downloading cover art, downloading on-demand AI karaoke vocal models, and delivering push notifications (see §3).
  • System settings & vibration (WRITE_SETTINGS, VIBRATE): WRITE_SETTINGS is used only if you choose "Set as ringtone"; gentle vibration is used for optional haptic feedback.

3Third-party network services

Network connections occur only when you use online features or when an unexpected crash occurs:

  • Push Notifications (Google Firebase Cloud Messaging - FCM): used to deliver push notifications for live radio station alerts and announcements. FCM processes ephemeral Device Registration Tokens solely for routing notifications. No personal data or listening history is sold or shared.
  • Deep Links & App Links: the app processes custom links (amuzicg://radio/) and verified web links (https://amuzicg-studio.web.app/radio/) to launch radio station player screens directly when clicked.
  • Crash Diagnostic Reporting (Google Firebase Crashlytics): if an unexpected app crash occurs, anonymous technical diagnostic logs (stack trace, device model, Android OS version, and Firebase Installation ID) are transmitted securely via HTTPS. This data is used exclusively for fixing bugs and monitoring app stability, and is automatically deleted after 90 days. No audio files, music metadata, or personal information are included.
  • Internet Radio Directory (radio-browser.info): searching or browsing radio stations sends your search term, country, language, or genre tag to de1.api.radio-browser.info to retrieve public station lists. No account or device ID is sent.
  • Radio Audio Streams: playing a station connects your device directly to that broadcaster’s streaming server, which receives your IP address (standard for internet audio streaming).
  • Album Art Lookup (Last.fm & Coil): artwork lookup queries artist and title text via ws.audioscrobbler.com. Downloaded images are cached locally on-device using Coil. On-device face detection (Google Play Services ML Kit) is used locally to center album cover crops.
  • On-Demand AI Karaoke Models: AI vocal separation models for Karaoke mode are downloaded on-demand from secure endpoints (https://akkisjoy.github.io/amuzicg-privacy/) and processed entirely on-device using ONNX Runtime.
  • In-App Updates (Google Play): queries the Google Play Store to check if a newer app version is available.

We do not include third-party advertising SDKs or commercial marketing analytics trackers. Diagnostic logging via Google Firebase Crashlytics is strictly limited to technical stability monitoring.

4Installed-app check (social audio, optional)

If you enable "Include social app audio", the app checks on-device whether a small, hard-coded list of messaging apps (such as WhatsApp, Telegram, Discord, and Signal) is installed. This is done solely to categorize and label audio files downloaded from those apps. This detection is performed entirely on-device and is never transmitted externally.

5Data retention & deletion requests

All app settings, playlists, and recordings are stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the app removes all local data (the app sets allowBackup=false).

Data Deletion Requests: Because we do not require user accounts or store user data on remote servers, there is no remote account data to delete. If you have sent us support or feedback emails, you can request the permanent deletion of your email correspondence at any time by contacting us at amuzicg@gmail.com. Deletion requests are processed within 7 business days.

6Children

Amuzick Player is designed for general audiences and does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13 (or applicable legal age).
