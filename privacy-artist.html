1Data we collect

World Artist Radio (amuzicg.musicplayer.artist) is built around privacy. You do not need an account or login to use the app.

  • On-device preferences: radio artist favorites, playback settings, sleep timer, and theme choices are stored locally via Android DataStore. Never uploaded to remote servers. Same privacy protections apply on Mobile and Android TV.
  • Feedback & support submissions (optional): if you send feedback in-app, your feedback message is transmitted securely to Firebase Realtime Database or sent via email to amuzicg@gmail.com solely to resolve issues.

2Permissions we request and why

  • Internet & network state (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): required to fetch global artist radio catalogs and stream live audio.
  • Notifications & background playback (POST_NOTIFICATIONS, FOREGROUND_SERVICE*, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED, WAKE_LOCK): playback controls, background audio playback, and optional push notifications for featured artist alerts and station updates.
  • Microphone (optional, RECORD_AUDIO): used on Android TV for voice search or live audio visualizer. Audio input is processed locally in real-time and is never recorded or uploaded.

3Third-party network services

  • Push Notifications (Google Firebase Cloud Messaging - FCM): used to deliver push notifications for featured artist radio stations and announcements. Ephemeral FCM registration tokens are processed solely for notification routing.
  • Artist Catalog Sync & Feedback (Google Firebase): Firebase Remote Config and Realtime Database are used to keep the global artist radio catalog synchronized and cache artist metadata locally. Optional feedback submissions are stored securely.
  • Deep Links & App Links: the app processes custom links (amuzicg://radio/) and verified web links (https://amuzicg-studio.web.app/radio/) to open artist radio player screens directly.
  • Crash Diagnostic Reporting (Google Firebase Crashlytics): if an unexpected app crash occurs, anonymous technical diagnostic logs (stack trace, device model, Android OS version, and Firebase Installation ID) are transmitted securely via HTTPS. Used strictly for technical stability monitoring and automatically purged after 90 days.
  • Station Directory (radio-browser.info & Firebase Catalog): searching or browsing queries de1.api.radio-browser.info or the Firebase artist catalog. No personal user identifiers are sent.
  • Radio Streams: pressing play connects your device directly to the broadcaster’s streaming server, which receives your IP address (standard for internet audio streaming).
  • Artist Images (Coil): artist avatars and station images are downloaded and cached on-device.

We do not use advertising SDKs or commercial marketing analytics tracking.

4Data retention & deletion requests

All app settings stay stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the app removes all local settings (the app sets allowBackup=false).

Data Deletion Requests: Because we do not collect personal profiles or mandate accounts, there is no remote account data to delete. If you have submitted feedback or contact emails, you can request the permanent deletion of your data at any time by emailing amuzicg@gmail.com. Requests are processed within 7 business days.

5Children

World Artist Radio is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.
