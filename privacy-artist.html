Privacy Policy for World Artist Radio — Top Global Singers, Musicians & Artists.

Contents1. Summary2. Data collected3. Third parties4. Retention5. Children6. Contact

1Summary

World Artist Radio (amuzicg.musicplayer.artist) is designed with privacy as a fundamental principle. You do not need to create an account, log in, or provide personal details to use the app.

2Data collected

We do not collect personal identifying information (PII). App preferences (such as selected radio theme or volume settings) are stored locally on your device.

3Third-party services

  • Station directory (radio-browser.info): browsing/searching sends only your query (artist, language, search text) to de1.api.radio-browser.info to return public station lists.
  • Radio streams: pressing play connects your device directly to that broadcaster’s server, which may see your IP address.
  • Station images (Coil): artwork is downloaded and cached on-device.

We use no advertising SDKs or third-party marketing analytics tracking. Google Firebase Crashlytics is used strictly for technical crash diagnostic reporting as described above.

4Retention & deletion

No server-side data exists to delete. Uninstalling removes on-device settings (app sets allowBackup=false). Emails you sent are deleted on request.

5Children

General-audience app; no personal data is knowingly collected from children because none is collected at all.
