Privacy Policy — Arya Satsang Vidhi

App: Arya Satsang Vidhi (amuzicg.vaidiksandhya) • Effective: 24 September 2026 • Contact: amuzicg@gmail.com

Arya Satsang Vidhi (Vaidik Sandhya) is an offline-first devotional app providing Vedic Sandhya mantras, Hawan audio, Swastivachan, Shantikaranam, Pravachan audio, Satyarth Prakash, and Bhajans. Everything is bundled inside the app for offline local playback — no user registration or account creation is required, no third-party ads are displayed, and we never sell or share your data.

1. Data We Collect

We collect no personal data and operate no remote data collection servers. All app settings stay strictly on your device:

  • On-device preferences: Your chosen app language (Hindi/English), text size preferences, and ambient video toggle are stored locally using DataStore/SharedPreferences. This data never leaves your device.
  • No tracking: No analytics SDKs, advertising IDs, user behavior tracking, or data-collection tools are embedded in this application.

2. Audio & Media Playback

All core audio mantras and ambient visuals are embedded within the application (res/raw and local assets) and rendered locally by ExoPlayer. Your listening history and playback state remain strictly local.

3. Device Permissions

  • Foreground Service / Media Playback (FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK): Enables continuous audio playback while the application is in the background and provides media controls in the system notification shade.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Displays the active playback control notification (Play, Pause, Previous, Next).

The application requires no network permissions for standard offline operation. Article viewers load bundled HTML files locally without transmitting data over the internet.

4. External Links & Play Store

The application includes no third-party ad networks or tracking services.

Rate / Review / Share: Tapping "Rate & Review" opens the Google Play Store application using standard Android system intents. No user telemetry is attached by our application.

5. Data Storage & Deletion

Because no user data is uploaded or stored on external servers, there is no server-side data to delete. Uninstalling the app or clearing application storage (Settings > Apps > Arya Satsang Vidhi > Storage) completely resets all local settings.

6. Children’s Privacy

Arya Satsang Vidhi is suitable for general audiences and children. We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

7. Updates to Policy

Any revisions to this Privacy Policy will be reflected in app updates and posted on this page with an updated effective date.

8. Contact Us

For questions or feedback regarding privacy, please contact: amuzicg@gmail.com.
