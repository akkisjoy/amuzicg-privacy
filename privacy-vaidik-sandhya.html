Privacy Policy — Vaidik Sandhya

App: Vaidik Sandhya (amuzicg.vaidiksandhya) • Effective: 24 September 2026 • Contact: amuzicg@gmail.com

Vaidik Sandhya is an offline-first devotional app that plays Vedic Sandhya mantras. Every mantra and the ambient diya video is bundled inside the app and plays locally — no account is required, no third-party ads are shown, and we do not sell or share your data.

1. Data we collect

We collect no personal data and we operate no servers. Everything the app stores stays on your device:

  • On-device only: your selected language (Hindi/English) and whether the ambient diya video is shown, saved with DataStore/SharedPreferences. This never leaves your device.
  • No usage tracking, analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs of any kind are included.

2. Audio & video playback

All mantras and the diya video are packaged in the app (res/raw) and played locally by ExoPlayer and VideoView. No audio, playback data or listening history is ever transmitted over the network.

3. Permissions we request and why

  • Foreground service / media playback (FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK): to keep mantras playing while the app is in the background and to show a media notification with playback controls.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): to show the playback notification (play/pause/previous/next).
  • Network (in-app browser only) (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): declared solely so the “Privacy Policy” link can load this page inside the app’s in-app browser (a Custom Tab — Chrome UI without leaving the app). The core app features never use the network.

4. Third-party services

The app integrates no third-party SDKs, trackers or services that receive data from it. The only network call is the optional in-app browser described above, which loads this policy page.

Rate / Review: tapping “Rate and Review” opens the Google Play Store in an external app. The app itself sends no information beyond the standard Play Store attribution that Android handles.

5. Data retention & deletion

Because we run no servers and collect nothing, there is nothing to delete server-side. Uninstalling the app removes your on-device preferences and caches. The app sets allowBackup=true, so if you have Android system backup enabled your preferences may be included in that backup; clearing the app’s storage (Settings > Apps > Vaidik Sandhya > Storage) removes them immediately.

6. Children

The app is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect any personal data from children, because it collects no personal data at all.

7. Changes to this policy

Any changes will be posted on this page and the effective date updated.

8. Contact

Questions or deletion requests: amuzicg@gmail.com.
