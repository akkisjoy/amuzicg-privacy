Privacy Policy — Amuzicg World Radio

App: Amuzicg World Radio (amuzicg.musicplayer.radio) • Effective: 24 September 2026 • Contact: amuzicg@gmail.com

Amuzicg World Radio streams public internet radio stations. No account is required, we show no third-party ads, and we do not sell your data.

1. Data we collect

We collect no personal data on our own servers — we operate none.

  • On-device only: favorites, quality filter, sleep timer and display preferences (DataStore). Never transmitted.
  • Feedback emails (optional): if you tap Feedback, your email app sends a message to amuzicg@gmail.com.

2. Permissions and why

  • Internet / network state (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): required to fetch station lists and stream audio.
  • Notifications / foreground service (POST_NOTIFICATIONS, FOREGROUND_SERVICE*, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED, WAKE_LOCK): playback controls and background playback.
  • Coarse location (declared): we do not collect GPS/location. Your country tab is derived on-device from SIM country code and device locale.

3. Third-party network services

  • Station directory (radio-browser.info): browsing/searching sends only your query (search text, country, language, tag) to de1.api.radio-browser.info to return public station lists.
  • Radio streams: pressing play connects your device directly to that broadcaster’s server, which may see your IP address (normal for streaming).
  • Station images (Coil): artwork is downloaded and cached on-device.

No analytics, crash-reporting, or ad SDKs are used.

4. Retention & deletion

No server-side data exists to delete. Uninstalling removes on-device settings (app sets allowBackup=false). Emails you sent are deleted on request.

5. Children

General-audience app; no personal data is knowingly collected from children because none is collected at all.

6. Changes & contact

Changes will be posted here. Contact: amuzicg@gmail.com.
